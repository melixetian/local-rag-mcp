# Business Travel and Expense Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-EXP-9  
**Owner:** Finance Operations

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses that are necessary for approved company work. Every claim must be submitted through LedgerFox within 10 working days after the trip or purchase.

An itemized receipt is required for any individual expense above EUR 25. A card slip showing only the total is not an itemized receipt. If the original receipt is unavailable, the employee must attach a missing-receipt declaration and obtain manager approval.

## Travel limits

Rail travel should be used for journeys under four hours when it is practical. Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer with manager approval. Business class requires approval from a vice president before booking.

The maximum reimbursable hotel rate is EUR 190 per night in Amsterdam, Paris, and London and EUR 150 per night in other cities. The limit excludes local taxes. A higher rate may be reimbursed when the conference hotel is required or Finance approved the exception before booking.

Personal minibar items, fines, leisure activities, and travel upgrades are not reimbursable. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a customer meal and must be reasonable in relation to the total bill.

Claims submitted more than 30 calendar days after the expense require written approval from the employee's department head.

# FlexNorth Remote Work Standard

**Standard ID:** RW-17  
**Owner:** Workplace Experience

Northstar Labs uses the FlexNorth hybrid-work model. Employees assigned to the Amsterdam office may work remotely for up to three days in a normal working week. Tuesday is the company-wide anchor day, and everyone must work from the office unless their manager has approved an exception in WorkHub.

Remote days are selected in WorkHub before 18:00 on the preceding Friday. The selection is intended for capacity planning and may be changed later with the manager's approval. Working from home does not change core collaboration hours, which are 10:00–16:00 Central European Time.

## Working from another country

An employee may work temporarily from another country for no more than 20 calendar days in a calendar year. The request must be created in WorkHub at least 15 calendar days in advance and approved by the employee's manager, People Operations, and Information Security. Approval is not automatic because tax, immigration, customer-contract, and data-access restrictions may apply.

Employees working remotely must use the company-managed laptop and connect through ZETA VPN before accessing internal systems. Public computers are prohibited. Confidential calls must not be taken where unauthorized people can hear them.

RW-17 does not create a right to permanent work outside the employee's country of employment. Permanent relocation follows a separate mobility process managed by People Operations.

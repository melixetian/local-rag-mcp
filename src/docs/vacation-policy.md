# Vacation and Personal Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-LV-204  
**Owner:** People Operations  
**Applies to:** All permanent Northstar Labs employees

## Annual allowance

Every permanent employee receives 24 paid working days of annual leave per calendar year. The allowance is credited monthly. Public holidays and weekends do not consume annual-leave days.

Employees may carry no more than 10 unused working days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days expires on March 31 unless People Operations approved an exception before the end of February.

## Request procedure

All vacation requests must be submitted in the Orbit HR portal. A request for five or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave. A request for one to four working days must be submitted at least three working days in advance.

The direct manager approves or rejects the request in Orbit. Buying tickets or booking accommodation does not count as approval. Employees should wait for the Orbit status to change to **Approved** before making non-refundable arrangements.

For an absence caused by illness, employees should use sick leave rather than annual leave. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive calendar day of illness.

## Emergency personal leave

Employees may take up to three paid working days per year for an urgent family matter. They must notify their manager in Slack or by phone as soon as reasonably possible and register the absence in Orbit within two working days after returning.

Questions about HR-LV-204 should be sent to `people-ops@northstar.example`.
